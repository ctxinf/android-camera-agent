Turn a spare Android phone into an on-demand camera.

• HTTP snapshots, MJPEG streaming, status and camera controls.
• MCP server for AI agents.
• Home Assistant integration through MJPEG and MQTT discovery.
• Releases the camera when idle to reduce battery use and heat.
• Runs locally without an account, analytics or a hosted service.

Grant camera permission, start the service and use the URLs shown in the app.
Use a trusted network or VPN; configure HTTP authentication before sharing access.
Separate Android 8.0+ and Legacy Android 4.0+ APKs are available.
